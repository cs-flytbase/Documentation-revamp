# Terminology

Rules for product names, spellings, and capitalization. Applied on every run.

---

- [terminology] "Verkos" is always capitalized. Never write "verkos" or "VERKOS".
- [terminology] "Detect Anything" is two words, no hyphen, when used as a standalone noun ("the Detect Anything agent"). Use hyphen only as a compound modifier ("Detect-Anything agent").
- [terminology] "FlytBase" — capital F, capital B, no space. Never "Flytbase" or "Flyt Base".
- [terminology] "Site Pointers" — always capitalized as a proper feature name.
- [terminology] "Flinks" and "Flows" — always capitalized when referring to the FlytBase features.
- [terminology] Dock variants: "Dock 1", "Dock 2", "Dock 3" — always capitalize Dock and use the numeral.
- [terminology] "Smart Missions" — always capitalized as a proper feature name.
